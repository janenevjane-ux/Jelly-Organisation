Jelly Organisation

Jelly Organisation is a youth-led non-profit association based in Rome, building non-formal education projects for young people through Erasmus+ Youth Exchanges. We work on breaking down cultural barriers between young people from different backgrounds, building intercultural understanding, and creating space for genuine connection across borders and languages.

The founders are also the creators of an interest-based association learning technique — a method for acquiring new skills and languages quickly by linking them to what a person already cares about, rather than through repetition or memorization.

OUR VALUES

Three ideas everything we build comes back to.

Not a mission statement — the actual filter every idea has to pass through.

01

Association

We don't memorise, we connect. Anything new sticks faster when it's tied to something a person already knows, feels, or cares about.

02

Weakness into strength

What looks like a weak point is usually just unused power. We build on what's already there — including the parts people try to hide.

03

Speed

Momentum over perfection. We'd rather move this month than plan for a year.
